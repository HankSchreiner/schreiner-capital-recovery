Privacy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Where our information comes from

We identify businesses and organizations from public unclaimed property records that states publish. We find contact details from information businesses make public, such as their own websites and public directories.

What we collect from clients

If you hire us, we collect what's needed to file your claim: contact details, the name of the person authorized to act for the business, and documents proving the business owns the funds. We use this only to prepare and file your claim and to communicate with you about it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't ask for passwords, bank logins or payment details.
  • We don't share your information except with the state agency holding your funds, as needed for your claim.

Opting out

If you'd rather not hear from us, reply "no thanks" to any message or email SchreinerCapitalRecovery@gmail.com, and we'll stop contacting you.
