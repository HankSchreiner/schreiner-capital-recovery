How it works

States hold this money until the rightful owner files a claim. Most businesses never check, and the claims process takes time and paperwork. That's the part we handle.

1

We find it

We search state unclaimed property records for funds reported in your company's name, including former names and old addresses.

2

We contact you

We tell you what we found and the approximate amount. You decide whether you want our help.

3

We file the claim

You sign a simple agreement that discloses exactly what the funds are. We assemble the claim and the supporting documents and see it through state review.

4

You get paid

When the state approves the claim, the funds are released, and our percentage is paid only then.

$0

upfront, ever. Our fee is a percentage of the money actually returned to you, agreed in writing before we start and never above the maximum your state allows.

Simple, contingent fees

Many states cap what recovery services may charge, and some set the limit at 10% of the amount recovered. We follow the rules of the state holding your funds, and the exact percentage is written into your agreement before we file anything.

If nothing is recovered, you owe nothing.

Questions

Straight answers.

Can I claim the money myself?

Yes. Every state lets owners search and claim their property directly, for free. We're a paid convenience: we find the money, assemble the documents, file the claim and follow up through approval. If you'd rather do it yourself, we're happy to point you in the right direction.

Is this legitimate?

Yes. Unclaimed property programs are run by state governments, and recovery services like ours are recognized by them. We never ask for payment up front, and our agreement states exactly what the funds are and where they're held before you sign. Be wary of anyone who wants money before your funds are returned.

Are you a government agency?

No. Schreiner Capital Recovery is a private business. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any state or government agency.

How long does it take?

States review claims carefully, and it usually takes a few months. Claims involving stock or older company records can take longer. We track the claim and keep you updated.

What do you need from us?

Usually a signed agreement, a claim form signed by an officer or authorized person, and documents showing the business owned the funds, such as a tax ID letter or records linking an old name or address to your company. We tell you exactly what's needed.

What if our company changed names or was acquired?

That's common, and often why the money was never found. Claims under a former name need extra records, and we help assemble them.
